# What the phone keeps

How what the phone keeps between launches is sealed before any store sees it,
and what it keeps. The code is `app-modules/seal`, the seal keys in
`app-modules/vault`, the health readings in `app-modules/health` and the
readings of where each stack stands on being up to date in
`app-modules/updates`, the readings of what each stack runs in
`app-modules/services`, each with its store under `src/Internal/Store`, how long
readings are kept in `app-modules/connection`, and the ports and values they
share in `app-modules/kernel`.

Each row says what the requirement asks and what in this repository answers it.
The spec is canonical; where this page and a requirement disagree, the
requirement is right and this page is a defect.

## Sealed, under a key the platform holds

| Requirement | What it asks | What keeps it |
|---|---|---|
| `N27-R3` | Everything kept is encrypted under a key in the platform's secure storage, readable only while the device is unlocked, and never under a key in the application's own files | `EncrypterSeal` seals with AES-256-GCM under a data key it asks `HoldsTheSealKeys` for; `PlatformSealKeys` keeps that key in the platform's store, asked for at `WhenAValueMayBeRead::WhileUnlocked`; rules `S4` and `S5` keep the encrypter and the `Crypt` facade in `Modules\Seal`; rule `A12` lets a store take and give nothing but what is sealed, and `WhatThePhoneKeepsIsUnreadableOnDiskTest` reads the database file after a summary is kept and finds none of it |
| `N27-R4` | A kept row names its stack only by a keyed hash, never by its identity, name or address | `SealedStack`, which `EncrypterSeal` builds as the HMAC-SHA256 of the stack's identity under a second key; rule `S6` keeps `hash_hmac` in `Modules\Seal` |
| `N27-R5` | With no secure storage the app keeps nothing between launches, and says so on App settings | `SealStanding` answers `Unavailable` and `Sealing` is refused with `WhyNothingIsSealed`, so nothing can be sealed and so nothing can be kept; `HowThisPhoneIsSet`, the App settings screen, asks `SecureStorage::isAvailable()` and, where there is none, says `settings.no_secure_storage` above every setting |

## Kept, and shown for what it is

| Requirement | What it asks | What keeps it |
|---|---|---|
| `N27-R6` | Where the key cannot be read, what was kept is deleted, a new key is made, every pairing stays, and the app says once that saved data was cleared | `ClearingWhatCannotBeRead` asks `Sealed::standing()` before anything kept is opened, and where it answers `MadeAfresh` asks every store through `ForgetsEverythingKept`, which the composition root registers under one tag; `YourStacks` says `connection.saved_data_cleared` on the first frame past the lock where anything was cleared. Pairings and sessions are in the platform's secure storage, which no store touches |
| `N27-R7` | A kept reading is shown on opening with when it was read, and replaced by a fresh one when it arrives | Health: `KeepingTheLastReading` keeps the newest summary per stack after each fresh one, sealed, and hands it back as a `WhatWasHeardSoFar` held as of when it was read; `HowThisStackIs::placeholder()` draws it on the first frame with its age, and the first summary the subscription carries replaces it. Updates: `KeepingTheLastUpkeep` keeps the newest reading per stack after each fresh one, sealed, and hands it back as a `WhatWasKeptOfTheUpkeep` with when it was read; `HowCurrentThisStackIs::placeholder()` draws it whole on the first frame with `health.summary.as_of`, `mount()` asks the stack, and the fresh reading replaces it and is kept in its place. Where the stack does not answer, the kept reading stays drawn with its age beside what stood in the way (`OpeningOnTheKeptUpkeepTest`). Services: `KeepingWhatItRuns` keeps the newest listing per stack after each fresh one, sealed, and hands it back as a `WhatWasKeptOfWhatItRuns` with when it was read; `WhatThisStackRuns::placeholder()` draws it whole on the first frame with `health.summary.as_of`, `mount()` asks the stack, and the fresh listing replaces it and is kept in its place. Where the stack does not answer, on the Services tab and on `WhatToDoWithThis`, the kept listing stays drawn with its age beside what stood in the way (`OpeningOnTheKeptListingTest`) |
| `N27-R8` | While a kept reading is shown and no fresh one has arrived, every control that acts on the stack is shown, not usable, with the reading's age beside it, and never hidden | Updates: while `WhatTheUpkeepTurnedOutToBe::$waitsForTheStack`, `how-current-this-stack-is` draws taking the update, and the confirmation's go-ahead, with `:disabled` and `connection.usable_once_the_stack_answers` beside each, and `wouldYouLike()` and `agree()` refuse; once a fresh reading arrives both are usable (`OpeningOnTheKeptUpkeepTest`). Services: the verbs live on `WhatToDoWithThis`, and while `WhatThisStackRunsTurnedOutToBe::$waitsForTheStack`, `what-to-do-with-this` draws start, stop and restart, for a service and for a form the kept listing names, and the confirmation's go-ahead, with `:disabled` and `connection.usable_once_the_stack_answers` beside them; `wouldYouLike()` and `agree()` refuse, no rehearsal is asked and no length is stated; once a fresh listing arrives they are usable (`OpeningOnTheKeptListingTest`) |
| `N27-R10` | Readings older than the kept period are deleted when the app opens and when the period changes | `LettingGoOfOldReadings::forgetTheOld()` in `connection` lets go of every reading older than `keptFor()`, asked by `YourStacks` on the first frame past the lock; `keepFor()`, which App settings calls with the operator's choice, lets go of them the moment it is kept; kept until removed, nothing is let go of for its age. It asks `ForgetsOldReadings`, which the composition root answers with `EveryStoreOfReadings` over every store of readings it registers, health's, updates' and services' (`LettingGoOfOldReadingsTest`, `ForgetsOldReadingsContractTest`, `OpeningOnWhatWasKeptTest`) |
| `N27-R13` | A kept reading in a shape this build does not read is discarded, and a kept setting is migrated to the current shape | Every kept reading is written in a `Shape`; `HealthReadingsInTheDatabase`, `UpkeepReadingsInTheDatabase` and `ListingsInTheDatabase` answer a row of a shape they have no case for as a `NewestReading` that cannot be read, and `KeepingTheLastReading`, `KeepingTheLastUpkeep` and `KeepingWhatItRuns` forget it, as they forget one that does not open or does not read as their reading (`KeepingTheLastUpkeepTest`, `KeepingWhatItRunsTest`). Every kept setting is in one sealed row, read by a `match` over every `Shape` in `TheSettingsAsKept`, so a shape a later build adds is an arm that carries the earlier one over; each setting is read on its own, and one it cannot make out reads as its standard and costs none of the others |
| `N27-R2` | The app keeps no repair offer, agreement, action the stack did not receive or confirmation of one, and no credential, session token or pairing material outside the platform's secure storage | Rule `A13`: `WhatThePhoneMayKeepTest` allows `Unsealed::of`, the one way a value becomes something to seal, only in each owner's writer (`TheSettingsAsKept`, `TheSummaryAsKept`, `TheNewsAsKept`, `TheUpkeepAsKept`, `TheListingAsKept`) and in `EncrypterSeal`, which hands back what it opened. It walks every type each writer is handed down to the types they hold, docblocks included, and refuses an `Offer`, a `Repair`, `Confirmed`, every agreement the kernel declares, `TakingAnUpdate`, an `IdempotencyKey`, a `Credential`, a `Session`, `Pairing`, `APairingCode` and `APairingLine`. Sessions and pairings are kept by `vault` in the platform's store, which no writer reaches |
| `N27-R1` | The app keeps only readings of a stack's health, what it runs, its updates and its household, the operator's settings, and per stack and kind an identifier of the newest item seen, and nothing else a stack sends | Readings of health, kept by `KeepingTheLastReading` in `health_readings`, and of updates, kept by `KeepingTheLastUpkeep` in `updates_readings`: `TheUpkeepAsKept` writes the reading the stack reported and not what became of the last update, and nothing builds the offer to take it or an agreement from what was kept until a fresh reading arrives; and of what a stack runs, kept by `KeepingWhatItRuns` in `services_readings`: `TheListingAsKept` writes the listing the stack reported and never an action, its confirmation or what a start waits on, and nothing agrees to a verb from what was kept until a fresh listing arrives. A marker in `news` holds what names and orders an item among its kind (a version, a number, or a check and when it began) and nothing the stack says about it (`TheNewsAsKeptTest`) |
| `N27-R19` | An item newer than the newest of its kind seen on a stack is new, where its kind is marked, and the first reading of a stack records what it holds as seen | `Noticing` in `news` keeps one marker per stack and kind, the newest item seen, sealed and named by the stack's keyed hash in `news_kept`; it finds what is newer by the order each kind is newer by (`TheItems`), records the newest as seen on a first sight, and raises the marker when an item is seen. `MarkingAsNew` holds which kinds a stack marks, all three until one is switched off, and a kind switched off forgets its marker (`NoticingTest`, `MarkingAsNewTest`) |
| `N27-R21` | A tab holding an item marked as new carries a mark a screen reader announces, and not by colour alone | `Listeners` hands back the stream's last `news` event beside its summary, read by `WhatIsNamedAsNewest` into `TheNewestNamed`. Every operator's screen about a stack holds the stack's stream through `HoldsItsStacksStream`, which `FindsItsWayAround` carries, while it is in front, and asks `Noticing::howMuchIsNew()`, which answers `HowMuchIsNew` through the same markers as What's new: an update marks Updates and a problem marks Health, a request marks no tab, the first reading marks nothing, and a kind switched off or unread marks nothing. Every screen that draws the bar hands `screen-closes` the marks it holds, which the component requires, and `screen-closes` draws each count as text in the tab's badge and gives the tab `news.new_on_tab` as `badge-label`. `scripts/patch_nativephp.php` lets `BottomNavItem` take that prop, and each renderer says it as the tab's name: Android's `contentDescription`, and iOS's `accessibilityLabel`. `MarkingTheTabsTest` and `NoticingTest` hold it |
| `N27-R16` | The app opens, after the lock, on the stack and tab the operator last used, and on the first stack in their order where that stack is gone | `TheOperatorIsHere` hands each screen that comes to the front to `NotingWhereTheOperatorIs`, which notes the stack and `WhichTab` of a screen under a tab in `WhereTheOperatorWas`, kept by `PlatformWhereTheOperatorWas` in the platform's store and written only when the place changes; `YourStacks::mount()` does the opening's housekeeping and then goes where `WhereAStackOpens::theOpening()` says, once a run: the stack last on view, else the first stack in the order, on its last tab through `TheWayAround::onItsLastTab()`, which the switcher and Your stacks open a chosen stack on too; an opening that cleared what the phone kept stays on the list, which says so (`OpeningWhereTheOperatorLeftOffTest`, `WhereTheOperatorWasContractTest`) |
| `N27-R22` | Nothing kept is drawn while the app is locked | `BehindTheLock` builds `Locked` in place of any screen while `TheLock` stands, so no screen that reads anything kept is built, let alone drawn; `Locked` draws the reason and the unlock and reads nothing kept; `NothingIsBuiltBehindTheLockTest` and `NothingIsDrawnBehindTheLockTest` |

## Set on App settings

| Requirement | What it asks | What keeps it |
|---|---|---|
| `N27-R9` | The operator sets how long readings are kept, from 1 to 365 whole days or until removed, and it is 30 days until they do | `HowLongReadingsAreKept`, `standard()` thirty days; `HowThisPhoneIsSet` offers `KeptFor`'s four lengths, until removed, and Other…, whose field `DaysAsked::typed()` reads and refuses outside 1 to 365; `LettingGoOfOldReadings::keepFor()` keeps the choice through `KeepsReadingsFor`, which `KeepingReadingsFor` keeps sealed in the phone's one settings row beside the lock's time away |
| `N27-R14` | App settings offers when to ask for the passcode again: immediately until the operator sets one, or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or 1 hour | `LockAfter` holds the five choices, `Immediately` its standard; `HowThisPhoneIsSet` offers them under Lock, `LockingAfter` keeps the one chosen sealed in the connection module's own settings row and tells the device through `DeviceAuth::allowAway()`, and `Locked` tells the device again each time the lock is passed, so a new process starts from the kept choice |
| `N27-R15` | App settings offers the order of the stacks, and every list of stacks follows it | `HowThisPhoneIsSet` draws Stack order under Stacks as `<x-design::order>`, the bridge's `Reorderable`, which `ReorderableRenderer` draws natively on each platform: a row is dragged into place, or moved one place with the screen reader's Move up and Move down actions, and `Reordering` says where it lands; `putStacksInOrder()` hands the order to `Stacks::putInOrder()`, and `PlatformStacks` keeps it in the pairings record, which every list reads through `configured()` (`EveryListOfStacksFollowsTheOrderTest`, `SettingHowThisPhoneIsSetTest`, `StacksContractTest`) |
| `N27-R18` | Each stack's settings offer which of updates, requests and problems are marked as new | `ThisStackOnThisPhone` draws Marked as new with a switch for each kind, all on until one is switched off, and turns a kind over through `MarkingAsNew` in `news`, which keeps the choice per stack (`ChoosingWhatAStackMarksAsNewTest`) |

## Taken off the phone

| Requirement | What it asks | What keeps it |
|---|---|---|
| `N27-R11` | Removing a stack from the phone removes every reading, setting and marker kept for it, in the same act | `ThisStackOnThisPhone`, the Stack settings page the menu ends a stack's part on, asks on the page and hands the stack to `RemovingAStack`, which writes the removal down in `RemovalsUnderWay` before anything goes, asks every `ForgetsAStack` keeper (the pairing first, then the session, the health readings, the reading of updates, the listing of what it runs, what is new, the words, the work left running and where the operator was), and strikes the removal off only once none keeps anything of it; `PlatformStacks` leaves a stack under removal out of every list, and `YourStacks` finishes a removal left unfinished when the app opens; the app then starts over on the next stack in the order, or on a first run (`RemovingAStackFromThePhoneTest`, `RemovingAStackOnItsSettingsPageTest`) |
| `N27-R12` | Clear saved data removes every reading, setting and marker the phone keeps, and no pairing or session | `ClearingWhatThePhoneKeeps` asks every store the composition root registers as `ForgetsEverythingKept` (the health readings, the readings of updates, the listings of what each stack runs, the phone's settings, what is new, the words, the work left running and where the operator was) and none that keeps a pairing or a session, then tells the device the lock is back to its standard (`ClearingWhatThePhoneKeepsLeavesPairingsTest`, `SettingHowThisPhoneIsSetTest`) |

## Asked for, and not answered

`N27-R1`, `N27-R7` and `N27-R8` are answered in part. The phone keeps readings
of health, of updates and of what each stack runs. A stack's household is read
afresh on every opening and kept nowhere, so its screens open on no kept
reading and draw no control beside one.
